## Amendments applied after the first audit (A-1 to A-8)

A-1 removed `maxSelections` and group `isActive`. A-2 removed `lastLoginAt` and the last-`staff.manage` guard.
A-3 removed domain-mismatch warnings. A-4 removed calendar-edit rejection. A-5 CSV reduced to name and email.
A-6 removed allergy warnings and snapshot. A-7 removed the staff debug panel. A-8 allowed a dish in many categories.
